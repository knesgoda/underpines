# landing and the legal pages should ever appear in a search index — member
# profiles, cabins, posts, camps, invite/join landings and the app itself
# are all off-limits to crawlers. A signed-out visitor sees the invite gate
# on every protected route anyway; this keeps handles and invite links out
# of search results and social unfurls entirely.

# This single wildcard group applies to every crawler (including Googlebot,
# Bingbot, Twitterbot and facebookexternalhit — do not add per-bot groups,
# because a crawler obeys only the most specific matching group and a bare
# Allow would re-open what this blocks).
